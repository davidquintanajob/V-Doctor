Comprehensive platform for veterinary clinics

V-Doctor System is a complete solution designed to centralize all clinical, administrative and accounting information of a veterinary clinic. It allows multiple veterinarians and technicians to work simultaneously on a single secure and consistent database.

The system consists of a server (API + database) and a mobile application from which staff access information in real time.

It integrates the management of clients, patients, medical records, drug inventory, sales, accounting, event calendar and users with specific roles and permissions in a unified platform.

System features

Each module is designed to cover a real daily need in veterinary practice, maintaining clinical traceability and administrative control.

Centralized clinical management

Patients, clients, medical records, vaccinations, deworming, surgeries, grooming and bathing services.

Detailed medical history

Complete record of consultations, evolution, weight, treatments and printable medical documents.

Inventory and medications

Stock control of medicines and products with direct impact on consultations and sales.

Integrated sales

Direct sales and sales associated with patients that are part of their clinical history.

Accounting in two currencies

Accounting management in CUP and USD with automatic conversion for accurate accounting.

Calendar and reminders

Events, vaccinations, check‑ups and reminders organized to improve patient care.

User management

Account, permission and role administration for staff and technicians of the center.

User profile

Customizable profiles for veterinarians, technicians and administrators with role‑based permissions.

Consultation method

Physical document for historical record or to print directly for clients.

Consultation and medical history

Complete view of consultations, evolution and related clinical documents by patient.

What do you need to use V-Doctor?

The system is designed to adapt to different infrastructures, from clinics with an internet connection to those that prefer a local environment.

Android mobile device

Each veterinarian or technician needs an Android phone or tablet to install the V-Doctor application.

Internet connection

For cloud synchronization. If there is no internet, a dedicated PC can be used as a local server.

Dedicated PC (optional)

A computer in the clinic that acts as a local server to operate without relying on the internet.

Optional: Printer (Epson recommended) to print prescriptions, medical records and tickets directly from the app.
